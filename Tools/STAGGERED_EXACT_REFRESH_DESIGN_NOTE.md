# AERIS23 Staggered Exact Refresh

Runtime basis: Witness-Bounded Affine Projection reduced exact projection/upload activity by roughly 88-94% and cut median BACK cost materially, but the 4.00 s affine freshness rail synchronized many Entry exact refreshes and produced periodic BACK spikes.

This successor keeps all affine visual and safety authority unchanged and distributes exact freshness deadlines across twelve deterministic 10 Hz-aligned slots from 2.80 through 3.90 seconds using stable FNV-1a of Entry CacheKey. The 4.00 second hard freshness rail remains unchanged and is never exceeded.

Acceptance focus:
- golden terrain/coast/contour appearance unchanged
- Runway Map Lock remains 0 px
- fixed 10 Hz authority unchanged
- affine witness acceptance remains <=0.08 render-target px
- mesh churn remains near zero
- steady READY exact Entry count per BACK becomes materially flatter
- `oh_stagger_back_peak` and `oh_stagger_back_gt8` remain low
- 80/160 km BACK p95 improves without materially regressing median
