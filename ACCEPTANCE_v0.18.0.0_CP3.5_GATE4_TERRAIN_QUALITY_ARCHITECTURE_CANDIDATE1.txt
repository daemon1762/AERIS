CP3.5 GATE 4 — TERRAIN QUALITY ARCHITECTURE CANDIDATE 1

STATUS AT PACKAGE CREATION
- Static/prebuild acceptance: REQUIRED and expected PASS before packaging.
- Native xbuild against the user's KSP 1.12.5 managed assemblies: NOT PERFORMED in package-creation environment.
- KSP runtime acceptance: NOT PERFORMED. Candidate 1 is not a runtime PASS until user test.

QUALITY CONTRACT
1. LOW
   - Persistent/authoritative FAR terrain: REAL 33x33.
   - Native presentation scale: 1.00.
   - No extra PQS sampling for presentation quality.

2. MIDDLE
   - Persistent/authoritative FAR terrain remains REAL 33x33.
   - No extra PQS sampling relative to LOW solely for quality.
   - Presentation uses a bounded higher-resolution GPU surface (logical VIRTUAL 65 class).
   - The implementation must not synthesize a heavier 65x65 terrain mesh from every 33x33 tile.

3. HIGH
   - REAL 33x33 FAR foundation always remains the readiness authority.
   - Only a bounded nearest visible subset may be transiently resampled from PQS at REAL 65x65.
   - Maximum transient REAL65 candidates: 4; >40 km: max 3; >80 km: max 2.
   - AERIS measured load may reduce the limit to 1 or suppress real65 refinement entirely.
   - A complete REAL65 FAR tile may be reconstructed on a worker to logical VIRTUAL 129 geometry.
   - Existing exact Route/Local payloads remain sparse exact overlays; missing exact payloads are not generated just for presentation.
   - Transient REAL65 must remain RAM-only and must not be written into the standard preload database.
   - Partial REAL65 commits must never replace a complete visible REAL33 foundation tile.

SAFETY / ARCHITECTURE BOUNDARIES
- Current preload/database format remains based on the established 33x33 FAR/Route/Local final contract.
- Whole-visible-area REAL129 PQS generation is prohibited.
- Whole-visible-area REAL65 generation is prohibited.
- Temporal presentation authority remains quarantined in Candidate 1.
- GUI.matrix temporal/world warp remains prohibited.
- Gate 3 Hotfix 1 ownship fixed-anchor and ownship-relative prediction authority must not regress.
- Wrong-range/orientation exact FRONT reuse remains prohibited.

RUNTIME ACCEPTANCE TARGETS
- LOW/MIDDLE/HIGH selectors visibly select distinct intended quality policies.
- MIDDLE should improve raster/vector edge presentation without meaningful PQS-load growth versus LOW.
- HIGH should eventually report bounded REAL65 refinement completion when runtime headroom allows.
- HIGH must not cause uncontrolled tile queues, multi-second repeated main-thread stalls, or a large persistent FPS collapse.
- Range sequence 160/80/40/20/10/5 km must retain correct ownship/prediction positioning.
- Palette V3 and runway/world symbology must remain aligned.
- Runtime logs must expose [CP3.5_GATE4_QUALITY] counters.

FAIL CONDITIONS
- MIDDLE triggers quality-only extra PQS grids across the viewport.
- HIGH attempts REAL129 PQS sampling.
- More than the bounded HIGH real65 tile count is requested at once.
- Transient REAL65 is persisted into the standard preload database.
- Partial REAL65 replaces the complete 33x33 foundation and creates holes/tearing.
- Ownship/prediction range-authority regression returns.
- Significant performance regression with no visible quality benefit.
