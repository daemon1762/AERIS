CP3 Gate 5 Candidate 14 — Solid-Surface Preload Exclusion Hotfix 1

Scope:
- Automatic terrain preload admits only bodies with a body-local PQS terrain controller.
- Stars, gas giants, and other surface-less bodies fail closed and are not auto-preloaded.
- Surface-less bodies are omitted from PRELOAD body presentation and cannot enter BUILD/RESUME/REBUILD generation paths.
- The rule is capability-based, not a Sun/Jool name whitelist, and therefore applies to mod bodies.
- Existing stored terrain files are not automatically deleted.
- Candidate 13 UI/preload fixed-policy behavior and runway/control boundaries remain unchanged.
