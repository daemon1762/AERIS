AERIS Flight Control v0.18.0.0 DEV CP2.5
INTEGRATED ACCEPTANCE CANDIDATE 3 — ACCEPTANCE CONTRACT

PURPOSE
- Preserve the accepted Candidate 2 lifecycle and Gate 1-4 baseline.
- Expose full Terrain Preload management in Main Menu, Space Center, VAB and SPH.
- Add a manual, non-persistent PRELOAD BOOST that temporarily uses every existing AERIS worker permit and an explicitly bounded main-thread PQS budget.
- Change ND and FDI factory/default policy to demand-driven AUTO and add absolute OFF.
- When SPEED is the only normal AP channel, AUTO FDI shows a speed-only panel that identifies VEL ONLY or ACC ONLY inside the display.

NON-FLIGHT PRELOAD CONTROL
- A persistent MainMenu bootstrap continues to tick Terrain Preload outside Flight.
- The non-Flight AERIS PRELOAD window reuses the complete Preload management page rather than a read-only status copy.
- Mode, speed, storage, idle timing, body priority/quality/cap and BUILD/PAUSE/RESUME/CANCEL/VERIFY/REBUILD/DELETE operations are available outside Flight.
- No AP, AA, FlightCtrlState or throttle authority is exposed by this surface.

PRELOAD BOOST
- Start and normal stop are explicit user actions; no setting, state file or automatic startup key exists.
- Flight entry safety-stops boost before any Flight-suspended work is processed.
- Boost assigns every worker in the already-created AERIS scheduler pool, removes the non-Flight safety reserve, unpauses archive capacity, fills a bounded preload compute queue and increases chunk-write parallelism.
- Worker priority is Normal only while boost is active and returns to BelowNormal afterwards.
- Main-thread PQS work remains bounded to 8-24 ms per update; it is not an unbounded busy loop.
- Boost does not create private threads, does not use Safety/LAND lanes and does not change CP3 terrain-payload residency.

DISPLAY DEMAND POLICY
- Factory and reset defaults are AUTO for ND and FDI.
- One-time display policy revision migrates the previous ALWAYS default to AUTO for installed Candidate 2 settings.
- AUTO / ALWAYS / OFF are available independently for ND and FDI.
- OFF is absolute: FDI provider/detail work and gauges are suppressed; ND display-owned snapshot/raster/GPU work is released without disabling Terrain safety state.
- SPEED-only AP demand opens FDI in SPEED GUIDANCE mode and labels AP SPEED — VEL ONLY or AP SPEED — ACC ONLY internally.
- Protection, terrain alert, traffic or external automation demand may still promote AUTO FDI to the full guidance view.

SAFETY / FROZEN BOUNDARIES
- Candidate 2 SyncModuleControlSurface lifecycle fix and CtrlSurfaceUpdate remain unchanged.
- No FlightInputHandler.state, MainThrottle, control-law, runway-coordinate or curated Track B change is introduced.
- Map DRAM remains metadata-only; Current-Body Resident Cache and terrain payload residency remain CP3.
