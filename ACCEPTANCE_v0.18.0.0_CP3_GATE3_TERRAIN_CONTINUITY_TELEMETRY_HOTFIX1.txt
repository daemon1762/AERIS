AERIS Flight Control v0.18.0.0
CP3 GATE 3 TERRAIN CONTINUITY & TELEMETRY HOTFIX 1

STATUS
- STATIC ACCEPTANCE CANDIDATE
- NATIVE COMPILE REQUIRED
- KSP 1.12.5 RUNTIME SMOKE REQUIRED

DEFECT CLOSED BY THIS HOTFIX
- BLACK WEDGE / BLACK FAN exposure while GPU terrain viewport coverage is PARTIAL.
- obsolete range requests generated by rapid manual 5/10/20/40/80/160 km changes.
- airfield startup maintenance incorrectly influencing Terrain AUTO quality/rate adaptation.
- missing persistent Current Body Resident Cache and Predictive Forward Corridor telemetry.

REQUIRED CONTRACT
1. Partial GPU terrain uses alpha-blended composition.
2. A compatible complete frame may back progressive output for at most 15 seconds.
3. If neither continuity nor CPU fallback exists, an explicit non-black unknown-terrain backing is used.
4. Complete requested-quality coverage alone may become the next continuity authority.
5. Manual range changes are latest-only and committed after 350 ms.
6. Obsolete GPU mesh work is cancelled when the coalesced range commits.
7. Airfield reload states are isolated from Terrain AUTO adaptation, including a 5-second recovery hold.
8. Resident/corridor telemetry is sampled at no more than 1 Hz and human-readable CP3 telemetry logs at 10-second intervals.
9. Performance CSV header and value cardinality must match exactly.
10. AA/AP/PROTECT/FlightState/LANDING/Integrations/Track B and Map DRAM remain frozen.
11. FULL BOOST remains absent and no new Flight safety-lane work is introduced.
12. UI tab, build-time generated identity, AVC identity and active acceptance runner all name this Hotfix 1.

DEFERRED
- Render Ready / GPU Ready residency-state promotion remains CP3 Gate 4.
- LAND control logic and Track B runway certification are unchanged.
