- scheduler dropped fixed at 1620

Root cause:
- AERISWorkerScheduler has a hard result queue cap of 256 and evicts the oldest result.
- FULL BOOST permits up to 176 active tiles and 12 pending blocks per tile (up to 2112 completion callbacks).
- Dropped terrain-block results never execute CommitBlock().
- TileState.PendingBlocks therefore never decrements.
- NextRunnableState rejects every saturated tile, leaving 176 permanently stuck states.
- Telemetry incorrectly reports PIPELINE BALANCED although the pipeline is deadlocked.

Immediate workaround:
- Do not use FULL BOOST in Candidate 4.
- If it has been started, STOP alone does not recover; restart KSP to clear the in-memory pipeline.
- Standard preload was functioning before FULL BOOST activation.
