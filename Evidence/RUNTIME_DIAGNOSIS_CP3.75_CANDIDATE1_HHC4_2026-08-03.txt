AERIS CP3.75 Candidate1 — HHC4 runtime diagnosis
Date: 2026-08-03
Decision: Candidate1 visual recovery is substantially successful, but runtime stabilization is required before performance optimization.

CONFIRMED FINDINGS
1. Late-CP3 contour/cartographic detail is visibly restored. Giant CP3.5 Candidate2 block mosaics were not observed.
2. Coastline/water-boundary stroke continuity is unstable during motion.
3. Frame cost rises with speed. Candidate1 telemetry showed ND repaint cost rising materially in the high-speed regime.
4. Candidate1 forced-recovery BACK rendering becomes a normal high-speed update mechanism. Runtime log examples:
   14:11:18 forced_recovery=6618
   14:11:23 forced_recovery=6644
   14:11:28 forced_recovery=6671
   14:11:33 forced_recovery=6703
   14:11:38 forced_recovery=6735
   This is roughly 5-6 additional forced BACK renders per second in that interval.
5. Root cause in Candidate1 renderer: direct FRONT compatibility rejected ownship displacement above max(25 m, range*0.0015). At 160 km this is 240 m, crossed in about 0.114 s at 2100 m/s. The recovery path ran before the existing latched-FRONT continuity path.
6. Track-vector geometry had a coordinate-authority defect. The line start used actual ownship position on the presented map, while endpoint/ticks were projected as if ownship were still at the map center. This necessarily stretches/collapses when FRONT is latched.
7. Track-vector time horizon also shrank with speed: seconds=clamp(range*0.38/speed,5,60). At 160 km / 2100 m/s this is below 30 s, so the 30/45/60 s marks disappear by formula rather than by rendering necessity.
8. LAND runway/localizer observation offsets are ownship-relative. Candidate1 projected them directly as map-center-relative values, creating the same coordinate mismatch whenever the presented FRONT center lags ownship.
9. Terrain quality AUTO/MEDIUM/HIGH and ND update AUTO/20/30/45/60 did not produce a useful visual-quality distinction in the tested Golden FAR path, while higher settings increased work. Candidate2 therefore consolidates runtime authority to Golden LOW and 10 Hz.

CANDIDATE2 RESPONSE
- Golden LOW only; legacy quality enum values become migration-only.
- Separate LAND terrain-quality preset disabled; LAND flight functionality itself remains untouched.
- ND user update-rate selector removed; internal presentation target fixed to 10 Hz.
- Scheduled FRONT refresh owns normal recentering; latched FRONT is presented before last-resort forced recovery.
- Projection refresh age reduced to 0.50 s to bound high-speed center lag without per-repaint rendering.
- Track vector endpoint/ticks converted to presented-map-center coordinates and stabilized to a 60 s horizon with range clipping.
- LAND runway/localizer geometry converted from ownship-relative observation coordinates to presented-map-center coordinates.
