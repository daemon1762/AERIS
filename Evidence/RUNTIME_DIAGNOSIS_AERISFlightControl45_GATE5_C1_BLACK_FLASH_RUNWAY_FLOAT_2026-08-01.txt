AERIS v0.18.0.0 CP3 Gate 5 Candidate 1 runtime diagnosis
Evidence: AERISFlightControl(45).zip + Video_2026-08-01_21-08-35.mkv
Date: 2026-08-01
Decision: CANDIDATE 1 REJECTED

Observed defects
1. Short ND terrain black/blank transition during otherwise normal flight.
2. Runway symbol can look like a floating/following I-shaped marker relative to terrain.

Runtime evidence
- CP3_GATE4C_VIRTUAL_DETAIL samples: 342
- DIRECT: 335
- BUILDING: 7
- CPU terrain draw remained 0.
- ready_build_violation remained 0.
- Examples of uncommanded/normal-flight BUILDING evidence:
  12:13:20 front=BUILDING range=80000-equivalent FAR state, back_foundation=0.857,
           ready_gf=16/30 required_gf=16/35.
  12:22:14 front=BUILDING range=160000-equivalent FAR state, back_foundation=0.900,
           ready_gf=20/72 required_gf=20/80.
  12:22:49 front=BUILDING range=160000-equivalent FAR state, back_foundation=0.986,
           ready_gf=16/71 required_gf=16/72.
- Additional BUILDING samples correspond to startup/range or explicit display-mode rebuilds.

Root cause
- Candidate 1 stops presenting an old complete FRONT when the requested projection moves
  outside its strict compatibility window, even though the replacement BACK has not yet
  completed. Because CPU fallback is correctly forbidden, the ND exposes the standby/build
  background for the transition.
- Terrain presentation and world-fixed ND overlays did not share one committed presentation
  projection. Overlays could advance with the requested/current projection while terrain was
  still showing a previously committed FRONT, making runway geometry appear to float/follow.
- At 40 km and above the runway body becomes very short while fixed-size endpoint threshold
  ticks remain visible, making the symbol resemble an I/H marker.

Candidate 2 corrective contract
- Keep the last complete same-body/same-generation GPU FRONT visible without affine/GUI warp
  while the exact next BACK is constructed; maximum latch age 8 seconds.
- Publish the exact committed FRONT projection and use it for Terrain-adjacent world symbology:
  runway/facility, traffic, trail, track vector and ownship map position.
- Alignment/map-lock telemetry must use the actually presented FRONT projection.
- Suppress runway endpoint threshold ticks and endpoint numbers above 20 km.
- Preserve CPU terrain draw = 0 and keep temporal GUI warp quarantined.
- If Terrain OFF released GPU resources, use normal ocean-map standby during OFF->AUTO rebuild
  instead of the near-black LAND background.
