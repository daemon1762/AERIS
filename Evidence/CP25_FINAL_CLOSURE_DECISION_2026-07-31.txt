AERIS v0.18.0.0 CP2.5 FINAL CLOSURE DECISION
Date: 2026-07-31

Project owner direction:
- Remove the manual FULL BOOST feature.
- Use the stable STANDARD Preload path as the only Preload mode.
- Close CP2.5 with this removal.
- Move development to CP3 Current Body Resident Cache.

Runtime basis:
- AERIS36 confirmed FULL safety and STOP recovery after downstream commit protection.
- FULL was slower than STANDARD and reduced frame rate.
- Therefore the complexity and performance cost did not justify retaining the mode.

Implementation result:
- FULL runtime API, scheduler policy, UI controls, state and telemetry removed.
- STANDARD commit-required and backpressure protections retained.
- CP3 payload residency remains absent from the closure baseline.
