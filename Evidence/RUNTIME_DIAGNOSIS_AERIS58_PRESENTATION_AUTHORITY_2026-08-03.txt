AERIS58 runtime diagnosis — 2026-08-03

Observed:
- ND terrain often remained ocean-blue/empty although the FAR foundation was complete.
- The terrain could appear briefly, proving supply/database was not completely dead.
- Runtime telemetry repeatedly reported front=DIRECT and temporal confidence=1.000 while the visible result was wrong.
- Later temporal diagnostics also reached max_error_px=Infinity, min_uv_margin=-1, drift=Infinity and heading_delta=180.
- direct_frames remained 0 because the Candidate 2 latch still routed through RenderTemporalReprojection instead of PresentFrontDirect.

Representative late log state:
- back_foundation=1.000
- ready_gf=20/103, required_gf=20/103
- temporal confidence=1.000, max_error_px=0.000, min_uv_margin=0.1000 during nominal periods
- history_frames_quarantined exceeded 12,000
- direct_frames=0

Root design defect:
The so-called exact latch did not directly present the committed Exact FRONT. It rebuilt/presented through the temporal presentation surface. Thus a bad/empty presentationTarget could hide a valid exact key frame.

Hotfix response:
- Exact FRONT becomes hard presentation authority.
- 1.25x overscan is cropped exactly back to the committed visible range around the ND anchor.
- Temporal remains shadow-only for diagnostics until runtime revalidation.
- NaN/Infinity is explicitly rejected.
- failed SetPass cannot clear the last valid presentation surface.
