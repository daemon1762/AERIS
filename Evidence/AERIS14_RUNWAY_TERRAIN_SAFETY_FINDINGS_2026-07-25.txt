AERIS14 VIDEO / LOG FINDINGS — RUNWAY AND TERRAIN SAFETY
Date: 2026-07-25

INPUTS REVIEWED
- Video_2026-07-25_11-14-05.mkv
- AERISFlightControl(8).zip (compiled mod and runtime logs)

CONFIRMED FINDINGS
1. MOD runway direction mismatch
   - KolaIsland RWY 34 registration heading and threshold-to-opposite-end bearing were approximately reciprocal.
   - The ND LAND funnel could therefore appear on the wrong side of the runway.
   - The same geometry would be unsafe as a future automatic-landing handoff.

2. GPU REL colour failure
   - Low terrain remained red while the aircraft climbed well above it.
   - The colour did not follow aircraft-to-terrain clearance.

3. GPU TOPO colour failure
   - User independently observed TOPO rendering defects.
   - Both failures shared the shader palette scale/offset dependency.

4. Coastline presentation
   - Land colour could interpolate into water across mixed land/water triangles.
   - Water and coastline were insufficiently distinct.

IMPLEMENTED RESPONSE
- Heading/bearing invariant and reciprocal endpoint normalization.
- Certification/ARM/Track Token rejection when geometry remains inconsistent.
- Correct-approach-side LOC/GS gating and N/A display.
- Explicit elevation-metre vertex colours for TOPO/REL.
- Fixed blue water, separate land/water clipped meshes.
- Land-only contours and dedicated thick coastline band.
- GPU AUTO capability fallback distinct from forced GPU ON.

NOT CLAIMED
- Native Mono compilation was unavailable in the creation environment.
- KSP/Unity/GPU runtime validation remains the user field gate.
