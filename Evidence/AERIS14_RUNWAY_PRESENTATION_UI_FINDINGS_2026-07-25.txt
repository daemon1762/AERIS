5. The top-right CLR SEL button overlapped usable map space. The requested order is ARM, CENTER, approach direction, SELECT, CLEAR.

Disposition:
- Fix off-scale pointer geometry and visual language.
- Move CLEAR to the preview panel immediately right of SELECT.
