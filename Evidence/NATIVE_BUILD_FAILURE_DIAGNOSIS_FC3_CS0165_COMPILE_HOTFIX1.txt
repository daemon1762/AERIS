OBSERVED NATIVE ERROR
Landing/AERISAirfieldRegistry.cs(1368,47): error CS0165:
Use of unassigned local variable `stored'

ROOT CAUSE
- `stored` was declared without an initializer.
- The condition used `witnessLibrary == null || RecordPlacementMismatch(... out stored)`.
- When the left side was true, C# short-circuit evaluation skipped the out assignment.
- The failure branch then read `stored`, violating definite-assignment rules.

FIX
- Initialize `stored` to `string.Empty` before evaluating the condition.

BOUNDARY
- Compile-only correction.
- No runway geometry, placement threshold, persistence, preload, UI, LAND or control behavior changed.
