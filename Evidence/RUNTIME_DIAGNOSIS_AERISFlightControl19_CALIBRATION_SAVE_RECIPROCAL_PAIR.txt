Additional design finding:
Two recorded endpoints define one physical runway, not one approach direction. The existing operational resolver already possessed A/B direction construction, but the persistence contract did not explicitly guarantee or validate the reciprocal pair. This hotfix makes the pair an explicit schema-3 invariant, forces both user-calibrated direction candidates available, validates endpoint reversal/180-degree headings/distinct IDs, and emits runtime evidence.
